How is my daily pace calculated?

Your monthly income, minus fixed expenses and savings, divided by the days in the month. Spending less than your pace rolls the difference into the following days.

I started using Pace in the middle of the month. Is that a problem?

No. Budgets run by calendar month. During setup, Pace asks roughly what you've already spent this month and takes it out of what's left, so your daily pace is right from day one. You can adjust that amount in Settings until the month ends; from the 1st, everything comes from what you log.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, with a backup in your own iCloud (on by default; you can turn it off in Settings). There's no account.

What does Pace Pro include?

The Apple Watch app, widgets, Control Center and Siri logging, receipt scanning, recurring expenses, your own categories, category limits, savings goals, the monthly recap, extra notifications and CSV export.

How do I restore or manage Pace Pro?

In Pace: Settings > Upgrade to Pace Pro > Restore purchases. To cancel or change a subscription: iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

I have a code. How do I redeem it?

In Pace: Settings > Upgrade to Pace Pro > Redeem a code.

How do I add the widget or Control Center button? (Pace Pro)

Widget: long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search "Pace". Control Center: open Control Center, tap +, then Add a Control, and search "Pace".

How does the Apple Watch app sync? (Pace Pro)

Your watch keeps its own copy, so you can log without your iPhone nearby. Changes sync automatically when they reconnect, or tap "Sync now" on either device.

How do I start over?

Settings > Erase all data.
